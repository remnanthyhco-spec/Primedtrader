Last updated: May 14, 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon use

Plain English summary: We collect your email, password, and trading session data to run PrimedTrader. We never sell your data. We use Stripe for payments and Supabase for storage — both are industry-standard secure services. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

Who We Are

PrimedTrader ("we," "us," "our") is operated from Sandpoint, Idaho, United States. We provide a futures trading preparation and education platform at primedtrader.com.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@primedtrader.com.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and operate the PrimedTrader platform
  • Power Coach Prime coaching and readiness scoring
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, billing receipts)
  • Improve our platform based on usage patterns
  • Communicate important updates about the Service
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase — an enterprise-grade database platform with row-level security, encrypted connections, and SOC 2 compliance. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified — the highest level of payment security certification available.

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.

Cookies

PrimedTrader uses minimal cookies and browser local storage to:

  • Keep you logged in between sessions (authentication token)
  • Remember your preferences (active strategy, selected firm)
  • Store temporary session data

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, but this will log you out of your account.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription, your account data is retained for 90 days in case you wish to reactivate. After 90 days, inactive free accounts may be deleted.

You can request immediate deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing support@primedtrader.com.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update inaccurate data through your Account Settings
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all data
  • Portability — request your session data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-out — unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email support@primedtrader.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

PrimedTrader is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at support@primedtrader.com.

International Users

PrimedTrader is operated from the United States. If you access the Service from outside the US, your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your data is the performance of a contract (providing the Service) and your consent. Contact us to exercise your GDPR rights.
